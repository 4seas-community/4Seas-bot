## What is 4Seas
<!-- also: 4Seas 是什么 什么是 社区 介绍 4Seas คืออะไร ชุมชน -->

4Seas is a community based in Chiang Mai, Thailand, running events around crypto, art,
and longevity. Members are mostly digital nomads, developers, and creators.
Everything happens through the events calendar: https://app.sola.day/event/4seas

## How do I join the community
<!-- also: 怎么加入 如何加入 加入社区 怎么参加 报名 门槛 申请 เข้าร่วม สมัคร -->

Events are open — pick one that interests you on Social Layer and show up.
There's no application process. The Telegram group is where day-to-day conversation happens.

## Where are events published
<!-- also: 活动 在哪 哪里 发布 日历 查看 有什么活动 กิจกรรม ที่ไหน ปฏิทิน -->

All events are on Social Layer: https://app.sola.day/event/4seas
Every evening at 19:00 the bot posts tomorrow's events in the group.
You can also send `/events` any time to see the week ahead.

## How do I host my own event
<!-- also: 办活动 举办 发起 创建活动 我想办 主办 จัดกิจกรรม -->

Create the event on Social Layer and file it under the 4Seas group. The bot's daily
digest will pick it up automatically — no need to tell an admin separately.

## Where do events usually take place
<!-- also: 地点 在哪里 场地 地址 位置 นิมมาน สถานที่ -->

Most events are around the Nimman area of Chiang Mai. Zuzalu Library is a frequent venue.
The exact address for any given event is on its Social Layer detail page.

## What can this bot do
<!-- also: 机器人 bot 能做什么 功能 怎么用 命令 บอท -->

- Posts tomorrow's events in the group every evening at 19:00
- Answers questions from this FAQ (`/ask`, or just @ the bot)
- Replies with useful info when common topics come up in conversation
- `/events` to check the calendar any time, `/faq` to see what topics it knows

The bot always replies in English, whatever language you ask in.

## How do I contact an admin
<!-- also: 管理员 联系 找谁 客服 admin ผู้ดูแล -->

@ an admin in the group. When the bot can't answer something, it will suggest this.
